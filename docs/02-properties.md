# Custom properties

Create these in HubSpot. Internal names are in `hubspot/properties.csv`.

Do not duplicate a property HubSpot already has (email, phone, company domain, close date, deal owner, amount). Map to the native field.

## Contact

- Lead Source
- Campaign Source
- Channel
- ICP Fit Score
- Persona
- Company Size
- Industry
- Country
- City
- Business Email
- Phone Number
- Last Contacted Date
- Last Outreach Type
- Preferred Contact Method
- LinkedIn URL
- Consent Status
- Do Not Contact
- Buying Role
- Decision Maker
- Interested In
- Last Meeting Date

## Company

- Industry

- Tech Stack

- Service Interest

- Location
