# Client monthly report

Send once a month. Do not put commission rates or internal payout status in the client copy.

```
Client Monthly Report
Client Name:
Reporting Month:
Prepared By:
Date:

1. Executive summary
- Overall performance
- What worked
- Biggest opportunities
- Key risks

2. Objectives and goals
- Lead target
- Meeting target
- Sales target
- KPI target
- Goal status

3. Performance summary
- Total leads generated
- Qualified leads
- Meetings booked
- Sales qualified opportunities
- Closed deals
- Revenue generated
- Conversion rate

4. Channel performance
- Outbound email
- LinkedIn
- Cold outreach
- Social media
- Paid campaigns
- Referrals
- SEO/inbound

For each channel: leads, cost, meetings, response rate, ROI.

5. Funnel performance
- New leads
- Qualified leads
- Discovery calls
- Proposals
- Closed deals
- Pipeline value
- Forecasted next month pipeline

6. Content performance
- Posts published
- Reach
- Engagement
- Clicks
- Website visits
- Form submissions
- Top performing content

7. Sales pipeline health
- Active pipeline
- Largest opportunities
- Average deal size
- Stage distribution
- Forecast

8. ROI and revenue snapshot
- Retainer value
- Generated revenue
- Cost of acquisition
- Net profit
- ROI percentage
- Estimated next month revenue

9. Recommendations
- Channel to increase
- Channel to pause
- Offer to test
- Audience to prioritize
- Messaging changes
- Creative changes

10. Next month plan
- Priority campaign
- Content plan
- Outreach goals
- Performance targets
- Focus areas
```

Example paragraph (replace every number from HubSpot before sending):

This month we generated qualified leads, booked discovery calls, and closed opportunities. Email outreach had the highest response rate. LinkedIn had the best-fit leads. Next cycle, increase the channel with the higher meeting rate and pause the channel with no replies.
