# Monthly dashboard

Build this in HubSpot reports. Looker Studio only if HubSpot cannot chart a metric.

## A. Lead generation

- Total leads generated
- Leads by source
- MQL count
- SQL count
- Meetings booked
- Discovery call rate
- Response rate
- Bounce rate
- Unsubscribes

## B. Sales funnel

- Pipeline coverage
- Lead to appointment conversion
- Appointment to proposal conversion
- Proposal acceptance rate
- Close rate
- Average deal value
- Average sales cycle length

## C. Retainer

- Active clients
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Retainer revenue forecast
- Renewal rate
- Churn rate
- Average client lifetime value
- Client satisfaction score

## D. Commission

- Total commission earned
- Commission paid
- Pending commission
- Commission by source
- Commission by account owner
- Commission by campaign

## E. Marketing execution

- Content published
- Social posts per platform
- Engagement rate
- Click-through rate
- Cost per lead
- Cost per booked call
- Lead-to-client conversion
- Channel ROI

## F. Operations

- Response time to new lead
- Tasks overdue
- Sales follow-up completion rate
- Renewal tasks completed
- Onboarding completion rate

## Formulas

- Response rate = replied leads / total leads
- Booked meeting rate = booked meetings / qualified leads
- Close rate = closed won deals / total opportunities
- MRR = sum of active retainer amounts
- ROI = (revenue generated - campaign spend) / campaign spend
- Average commission = total commission / closed won deals

Leave a formula blank when the denominator is zero.
